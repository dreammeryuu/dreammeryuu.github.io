Jarvis

A private, single-user voice assistant · 私人语音管家（仅本人使用）

Jarvis is a personal voice assistant that runs on its owner's own computers at home. It is not a public service and has no other users. With the owner's permission it can read (read-only) his Gmail and Google Calendar so he can ask things like "what's on my calendar today" or "any important unread email".

Jarvis 是运行在主人自己家里电脑上的私人语音管家，不对外提供服务，没有其他用户。经主人授权，它可以只读地查看他的 Gmail 和 Google 日历，用来回答"今天有什么安排""有没有要紧的未读邮件"这类问题。
